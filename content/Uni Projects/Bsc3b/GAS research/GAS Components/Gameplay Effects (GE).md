- Used to modify [[Attributes]] and Gameplay Tags on owned or external ASCs.
- Their time-span can be: 'Instant', has 'Duration' or 'Infinite'.
- They feature a variety of Custom Calculation settings, such as capturing attributes from the Source/Target's ASC to act as parameters before application.
- Rarely instantiated, instead [[Gameplay Effect Spec]] are used.
