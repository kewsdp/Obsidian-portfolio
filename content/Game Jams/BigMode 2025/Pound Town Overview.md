This was a submission for Bigmode 2025, with the theme "Power". We decided to create a sandbox experience, featuring the unhinged freedom seen in games like 'Postal', within the Godot engine. I was tasked with creating an interactable NPC with wandering behavior, that when provoked, would follow the player and attack them (with the stretch goal of ragdoll physics) This was my first time working with the Godot engine, therefore I wanted to keep my goals simpler to manage time constraints.
