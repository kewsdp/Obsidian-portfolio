This was a submission for a University Vertical Game Jam during my second year. It was setup in correspondence with members of the Norwich Sainsbury Centre, and required teams to make a game involving the various exhibits.

Our decided direction was to make a game in which the player must recover these artifacts by traversing into paintings (also real exhibits) and completing various quests from the residents within. My task, was to make an inventory system for the various items the player collects throughout the game.
